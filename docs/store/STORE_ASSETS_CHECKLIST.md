## Screenshots still required

Capture screenshots from the real v1.4.0 extension before submission.

### Microsoft Edge Add-ons

- accepted sizes: 640×480 or 1280×800;
- prepare English and Simplified Chinese screenshots for their respective listing languages;
- suggested scenes: Current page, Batch download/control center, and verification assistant.

### Chrome Web Store

- recommended size: 1280×800;
- 640×400 is also supported;
- upload localized screenshots in the Store Listing locale selector where useful.

Before capturing screenshots, remove personal account names, institutional information, local paths, private paper lists, and unrelated browser tabs.
